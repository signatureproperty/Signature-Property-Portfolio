Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: October 2026 · Signature Property & Consultant (Lahore, PK)

1. Scope & Objective

Signature Property & Consultant ("Signature Property", "we", "our") respects the privacy and confidentiality of prospective buyers, sellers, tenants, and institutional developers who interact with our firm. This statement outlines how client contact details and property inquiry data are treated.

2. Information We Receive

When you initiate contact through our portfolio website, direct forms, email, or official WhatsApp dispatchers, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact phone / WhatsApp number
  • Your geographic location or residency jurisdiction (e.g. Overseas Pakistanis in the UK, UAE, USA)
  • Specific property requirements, spatial preferences, or budget parameters
  • Technical metadata (browser user-agent and basic diagnostics necessary for secure communication)

3. How Your Information is Used

Information provided by clients is utilized strictly to facilitate real estate advisory discussions, schedule property inspections, deliver video walkthroughs, and coordinate private purchase negotiations. We do not sell, rent, or trade client personal information to marketing databases or third-party lead aggregators.

4. WhatsApp & Remote Communication

When utilizing WhatsApp for consultations, messages are transmitted through Meta's end-to-end encrypted protocol. Clients are advised never to transmit bank login passwords, credit card PINs, or sensitive non-public credentials over unsecured web forms.

5. Contact & Data Access

To review, update, or request deletion of contact information held by our consultancy, please write to our executive desk at thesignatureproperty@gmail.com or call +92 329 8838068.
